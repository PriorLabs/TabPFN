On torch 2.14, `TorchQuantileTransformer` stopped matching sklearn's quantiles: torch now derives the sample index for a quantile in double precision, so the float32 quantile positions landed just short of an exact order statistic and got interpolated instead. Where two neighbouring samples were close together, that shifted transformed values by up to 1e-4. The fit is now computed in float64, so results match sklearn again on both torch 2.13 and 2.14; quantiles from float32 fits move by ~1e-7 towards sklearn's values.
