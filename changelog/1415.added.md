`TabPFNClassifier.fit` and `TabPFNRegressor.fit` take experimental `sample_weight` and `label_reliability` arguments, applied by models whose inference config sets `SAMPLE_WEIGHT_MODE`. For every other model `fit` raises when they are given, which includes `BaggingClassifier` and `BaggingRegressor`: they now pass their bootstrap counts as `sample_weight` instead of fitting on a row subset.
