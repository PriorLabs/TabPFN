The `"zero_inflated"` preset of `TabPFNRegressor` now sets `REGRESSION_TARGET_SIGN_FLIP=False` and `AVERAGE_BEFORE_SOFTMAX=False`. Before, it kept the checkpoint's values. With both on, members saw zeros away from the fixed zero atom, and logit averaging removed most of the ensemble's mass at zero: the preset predicted too few zeros and too high a mean. A key in `inference_config` or an `average_before_softmax` argument still wins.
