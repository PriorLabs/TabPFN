`batch_ensemble_members=True` on `TabPFNClassifier` and `TabPFNRegressor` (with `fit_mode="fit_preprocessors"` on a single device) runs ensemble members whose prepared inputs have the same shape as one batched forward pass, bounded to tables of at most 8,000 rows, 8 members and 8e6 input elements per forward and an estimated 20 GiB of activation memory. Small-table inference gets up to about 2x faster; under autocast the outputs differ from the per-member pass by up to about 1e-3 in probability.
