`n_preprocessing_jobs > 1` now runs the per-estimator preprocessing on threads instead of processes. The process pool copied the training table into every worker and every transformed table back, so it was slower than `n_preprocessing_jobs=1` at every table size (4x slower on 1M rows) and paid 1.5 to 3 s per pool start. Threads share the data: fit-time preprocessing of a 1M x 30 table drops from 3.3 s to 1.3 s with 8 jobs. The default stays 1 and predictions are unchanged.
