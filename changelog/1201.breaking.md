`scikit-learn>=1.4.2`, `pandas>=1.5.3` and `numpy>=1.23.5` are now the minimum supported versions, raised to match the new `skrub` dependency. A column of numbers stored as strings is now read as numbers regardless of any setting, which changes predictions on tables that have one. `InferenceConfig.USE_DATES` and `USE_TEXT` turn on date expansion and text encoding, both off by default; turning either on changes predictions on tables holding dates or text accordingly, but `n_features_in_` and `feature_names_in_` always describe the frame passed to `fit()`, regardless of either setting.
