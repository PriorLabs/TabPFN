`scikit-learn>=1.4.2`, `pandas>=1.5.3` and `numpy>=1.23.5` are now the minimum supported versions, raised to match the new `skrub` dependency. A column of numbers stored as strings is now read as numbers regardless of any setting, which changes predictions on tables that have one. `use_dates` and `use_text` turn on date expansion and text encoding, both off by default; turning either on grows `n_features_in_` and `feature_names_in_`, since they describe the converted frame, and changes predictions on tables holding dates or text accordingly.
