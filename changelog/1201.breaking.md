`scikit-learn>=1.4.2`, `pandas>=1.5.3` and `numpy>=1.23.5` are now the minimum supported versions, raised to match the new `skrub` dependency. Two consequences of the input type handling added in this release: `n_features_in_` and `feature_names_in_` describe the converted frame, so they grow when a datetime or text column is expanded, and predictions change on tables holding dates, text, or numbers stored as strings. `use_dates=False` and `use_text=False` turn off the date and text handling, but a column of numeric strings is still read as numbers.
