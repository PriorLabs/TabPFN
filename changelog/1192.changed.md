The Yeo-Johnson target transforms for regression (`power`, `safepower`) now see the target divided by its scale (the median of `|y|`) rather than in its original units. Yeo-Johnson bends at zero with a fixed unit offset, so on a target far from unit scale it degenerates into a linear map that removes no skew at all, which made the model's accuracy depend on whether the target was measured in, say, units or millions. The zero point the transform is anchored to is preserved, so a positive target stays positive; only the units stop mattering. Box-Cox presets are unaffected, as they already rescale their input.
