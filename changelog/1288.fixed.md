Regression density and likelihood evaluation no longer reports infinite NLL for targets in small buckets. The bar-distribution border remap computed each destination bucket's mass by differencing a cumulative sum in float32, where the resolution near a CDF of 1 is ~6e-8, so any smaller bucket became exactly zero and `-inf` after the `log()`. The differencing now runs in float64, the two outer buckets are read as the half-normal tails `FullSupportBarDistribution` actually puts there instead of being pinned to 0 and 1 (which dropped that mass at any precision whenever the destination grid was wider than the source), buckets inside a tail are computed in closed form rather than by differencing a CDF that has rounded to 1.0, and the ensemble is pooled in log space so no bucket mass is lost to float32 on the way in. Point predictions and CRPS are unchanged. With `eval_metric="nll"`, temperature tuning previously skipped every candidate whose holdout loss was infinite and silently fell back to the default temperature; that sweep now discriminates, so a tuned NLL temperature may differ.
