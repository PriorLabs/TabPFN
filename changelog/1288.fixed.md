Regression density and likelihood evaluation reports far fewer infinite NLLs for targets that fall in a narrow bucket or in a distribution's tail, and the two tails of the predictive distribution are now treated alike. Point predictions shift with it: by round-off for well-behaved targets, and measurably where an ensemble member's target transform narrows its grid inside the shared one, because that member's tail mass is now spread along its half-normal instead of piled into the single bucket straddling its border. With `eval_metric="nll"`, temperature tuning previously discarded every candidate whose holdout loss came out infinite and silently kept the default temperature, so a tuned NLL temperature may now differ.
