`inference_config={"TRANSFORM_DATES": True}` expands a column holding a genuine datetime dtype (`datetime64`, tz-aware, or `period`) into calendar features: the year, the day of year, the seconds since the epoch, and the cyclical month, day and weekday pairs, plus the time of day when the column carries one. Off by default, in which case such a column is refused with a `TabPFNValidationError` naming it. Not run by the fine-tuning estimators.
