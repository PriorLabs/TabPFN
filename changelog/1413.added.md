Add `TabPFNRegressor(presets=...)`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. The `"zero_inflated"` preset is for nonnegative targets with many exact zeros. It sets the `nonnegative_and_capped` readout, and the `log_fixed_atom` target transform for models with a `TabPFNV4Config` or `log_quantile_gap` for all others. `fit` suggests it, once per process, for nonnegative targets with more than 20% zeros and at least 50 widely spread, non-count positives. A checkpoint that stores its own zero-inflated target transform keeps it.
