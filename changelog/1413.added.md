Add `TabPFNRegressor(presets=...)`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. The `"zero_inflated"` preset is for nonnegative targets with many exact zeros: it sets the `log_fixed_atom` target transform and the `nonnegative_and_capped` readout. On the models up to TabPFN-3.5 it sets `log_quantile_gap` with the same readout instead. `fit` suggests the preset, once per process, for nonnegative targets with more than 20% zeros and at least 50 widely spread, non-count positives.
