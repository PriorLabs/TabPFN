Add `TabPFNRegressor(presets=...)`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. The `"zero_inflated"` preset is for nonnegative targets with many exact zeros. It sets the `nonnegative_and_capped` readout, and the `log_quantile_gap` target transform for the models up to TabPFN-3.5 or `log_fixed_atom` for other models. `fit` suggests it, once per process, for nonnegative targets with more than 20% zeros and at least 50 widely spread, non-count positives. A checkpoint that stores its own zero-inflated target transform keeps it.
