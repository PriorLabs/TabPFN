Add `TabPFNRegressor(presets=...)`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. The `"zero_inflated"` preset sets the `log_fixed_atom` target transform and the `nonnegative_and_capped` readout for nonnegative targets with many exact zeros, and `fit` suggests it for nonnegative targets with more than 20% zeros. A checkpoint that stores its own zero-inflated target transform keeps it.
