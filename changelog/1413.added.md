Add `TabPFNRegressor(presets=...)`, named bundles of `inference_config` values. The `"zero_inflated"` preset is for nonnegative targets with many exact zeros. It sets the `nonnegative_and_capped` readout and `REGRESSION_Y_PREPROCESS_TRANSFORMS="zero_inflated_auto"`, a new value that picks a zero-inflated target transform for the model: the checkpoint's own, else `log_quantile_gap` for the models up to TabPFN-3.5 or `log_fixed_atom` for others. `fit` suggests the preset, once per process, for nonnegative targets with more than 20% zeros and at least 50 widely spread, non-count positives.
