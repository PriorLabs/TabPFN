`fit` now accepts `sample_weight`, so scikit-learn meta-estimators pass weights to it, and `fit` raises for models without `SAMPLE_WEIGHT_MODE`. `BaggingClassifier(TabPFNClassifier())` and `BaggingRegressor(TabPFNRegressor())` raise in `fit` with any `bootstrap` and `max_samples`, because they pass their row counts as weights instead of fitting on a row subset. `CalibratedClassifierCV(TabPFNClassifier()).fit(X, y, sample_weight=w)` raises instead of warning that the estimator ignores the weights.
