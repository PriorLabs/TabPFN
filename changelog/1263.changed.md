Faster ordinal encoding of categorical columns: numeric arrays are encoded with a numpy-based encoder instead of sklearn's `OrdinalEncoder`, whose input validation dominated the step on small tables and is paid once per ensemble member. On a 598 x 31 table with 25 categorical columns the step goes from 3.97 ms to 1.20 ms per fit and from 1.59 ms to 0.27 ms per transform. Preprocessed outputs are unchanged.
