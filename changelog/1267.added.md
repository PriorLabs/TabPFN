`kv_cache_at_predict=True` on `TabPFNClassifier` and `TabPFNRegressor` (for `fit_mode="low_memory"` and `"fit_preprocessors"`) builds each ensemble member's key-value cache from the training rows at predict time, runs the test rows through it in chunks of `TABPFN_MAX_BATCHED_TEST_ROWS` and drops the cache, bounding test-side device memory without keeping caches between calls. Predict time is unchanged from about 2,000 rows up; the cache keeps the computed dtype unless `kv_cache_precision` is given.
