Attention backends can tell the in-context train call apart from every other call: it carries `AttentionRole.ICL_TRAIN`, on `AttentionSpec.role` and as `role=` to `run`. Backends that write to its keys and values in place, as the FP8 backend in TabPFN Plus does, no longer take the many-class decoder call when a prediction has as many test rows as training rows.
