`categorical_features_indices` is defined against the frame passed to `fit()`, but a column expanding under `USE_DATES` or `USE_TEXT` shifts every column after it, so a declared index could end up pointing at the wrong column. Declared indices are now translated to their position after conversion, by column name. A declared index whose own column is the one that expands is dropped rather than misapplied, since it no longer exists as a single column.
