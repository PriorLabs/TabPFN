A fit input that would be converted whole is validated and cleaned a range of columns at a time, into a float32 table the ensemble members share when they all read float32 (as under GPU preprocessing). This cuts the host memory of tables with integer, boolean, nullable, categorical, string, date or float64 columns. Predictions stay those of a float64 table: which columns are constant and the feature-importance order are decided on the input before it is narrowed, and codes float32 cannot hold keep a float64 table.
