Add the `REGRESSION_READOUT` options `"nonnegative_and_pareto_tail"` and `"nonnegative_and_exponential_tail"`. Their mean replaces the predicted distribution above a threshold u with a generalized Pareto tail fitted to the training targets above u and capped at an upper threshold M. `InferenceConfig.REGRESSION_TAIL_THRESHOLD` sets u, `InferenceConfig.REGRESSION_TAIL_CAP` sets M (default: twice the largest training target), and `RegressorTuningConfig.rescale_tail_probability` fits the tail probability on the tuning holdout rows.
