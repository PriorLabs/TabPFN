`InferenceConfig.AVERAGE_BEFORE_SOFTMAX` lets a checkpoint declare whether the ensemble averages logits before the softmax or averages probabilities. `average_before_softmax` now defaults to `"auto"` on `TabPFNClassifier` and `TabPFNRegressor`, meaning "use the checkpoint's value"; checkpoints without the field keep averaging probabilities, as before. An explicit `average_before_softmax` still wins; naming a value through both the argument and `inference_config`, or ensembling checkpoints that disagree on it, raises.
