A column carrying pandas' `category` dtype is read as categorical whatever its number of distinct values, like one listed in `categorical_features_indices`, and is handed to the tuning estimators as such. It used to be classified as text above `MIN_CARDINALITY_FOR_TEXT`, since validation had already flattened the dtype away by the time modality detection ran. `inference_config={"CATEGORY_DTYPE_IS_CATEGORICAL": False}` restores the old reading.
