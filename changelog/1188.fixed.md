Fitting on a dataframe containing a `datetime64` column no longer raises. Depending on the other columns it previously failed with a dtype promotion error during input validation, with `ValueError: Invalid dtype for X` while cleaning, or with a promotion error inside the ordinal encoder. Datetime columns are now expanded into numeric calendar features, namely the year, the day of year, the seconds since epoch and cyclical month, day and weekday pairs, plus the time of day when the column carries one.
