`InferenceConfig.N_ESTIMATORS` lets a checkpoint declare how many estimators to run, and means exactly what the `n_estimators` argument means: an int is used as given and never raised for feature coverage, `"auto"` (the default) means 8 and may be raised. `n_estimators="auto"` now takes the checkpoint's value, so checkpoints without the field behave as before. An explicit `n_estimators` still wins; naming a count both ways, or ensembling checkpoints that disagree on it, raises.
