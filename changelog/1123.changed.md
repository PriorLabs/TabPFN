Raised the default CPU sample limit from 1000 to 5000. With bf16 autocast on modern CPUs, inference on datasets up to 5000 rows now runs in a reasonable time (~30s at 5000 rows). The override (`ignore_pretraining_limits=True` / `TABPFN_ALLOW_CPU_LARGE_DATASET=1`) and the ">200 samples may be slow" warning are unchanged.
