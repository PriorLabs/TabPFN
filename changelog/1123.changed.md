Raised the default CPU sample limit from 1000 to 5000 for the v3 model (other versions keep the 1000 limit). With bf16 autocast on modern CPUs, v3 inference on datasets up to 5000 rows now runs in a reasonable time (~30s at 5000 rows). The override (`ignore_pretraining_limits=True` / `TABPFN_ALLOW_CPU_LARGE_DATASET=1`) and the ">200 samples may be slow" warning are unchanged.
