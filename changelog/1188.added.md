`fit()` now handles datetime and text columns instead of leaving them to the ordinal encoder. Columns are given the type their contents imply before validation, using [skrub](https://skrub-data.org), which becomes a dependency: a column of `"39.00"` arrives as a number, a datetime column is expanded into the year, the day of year, the seconds since epoch and cyclical month, day and weekday pairs, and a text column is encoded with tf-idf over character n-grams followed by a truncated SVD. Numeric columns and low-cardinality strings are passed through, so categorical detection and encoding are unchanged. Three new parameters control it: `use_dates` and `use_text`, both `True`, and `text_cardinality_threshold` (default 40), the number of distinct values above which a string column is read as text rather than as a category. The fitted converter is available on the estimator as `input_converter_`.
